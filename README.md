# ☕ Coffee Sales Performance & Customer Analytics
Interactive Excel dashboard analyzing transactional coffee sales data across product varieties, customer tiers, and regional markets to track revenue growth, identify high-value customer segments, and benchmark geographic demand.

📊 Key Features

Time-Series Sales Tracking: Monthly and annual revenue trajectories broken down by coffee bean variety (Arabica, Excelsa, Liberica, Robusta)

Geographic Market Comparison: Country-level revenue distribution benchmarking sales across the US, Ireland, and the UK

Top Customer Analysis: Ranked revenue contribution to pinpoint high-LTV buyers

Interactive Slicers & Timelines: Dynamic filtering by roast type, package size, loyalty card status, and order date

📂 Dataset
Relational coffee retail sales dataset sourced from GitHub (1,000 orders, 1,000 customers, 48 SKUs).

🛠 Tools
Excel (XLOOKUP, INDEX/MATCH, Pivot Tables, Slicers, Dynamic Timelines)

📌 Skills
Dashboard Design • Relational Data Modeling • Sales Analytics • Data Transformation • KPI Reporting
