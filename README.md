# ☕ Coffee Sales Performance & Customer Analytics

Interactive Excel dashboard modeling 1,000+ relational transactions across product varieties, customer tiers, and regional markets to track revenue growth, identify high-value customer segments, and benchmark geographic demand.

## 📊 Key Features
- **Time-Series Sales Tracking:** Monthly and annual revenue trajectories broken down by coffee bean variety (Arabica, Excelsa, Liberica, Robusta).
- **Geographic Market Comparison:** Country-level revenue distribution benchmarking sales across the US, Ireland, and the UK.
- **Top Customer Analysis:** Ranked revenue contribution to pinpoint high-LTV buyers.
- **Interactive Slicers & Timelines:** Dynamic filtering by roast type, package size, loyalty card status, and order date.

## 📂 Dataset
Relational coffee retail sales dataset sourced from GitHub/Kaggle (1,000 orders, 1,000 customers, 48 SKUs).

## 🛠 Tools
- **Microsoft Excel:** XLOOKUP, INDEX/MATCH, Dynamic Arrays, Nested Logic
- **Data Modeling & BI:** Multi-Table Relational Schema, Pivot Tables, Dynamic Timelines & Slicers
